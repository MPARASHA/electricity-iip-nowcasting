Holidays to be observed by Central Government Offices in Kerala in 2024


Posted On: 14 NOV 2023 5:24PM by PIB Thiruvananthpuram

The list of Holidays for the year 2024 to be observed by Central Government Offices in Kerala has been published. In 2023, there will be 17 holidays and 43 restricted holidays.

Holidays:

January 26

 

Republic Day

March 08

 

MahaShivarathri

March 29

 

Good Friday

*April 10

 

Idu'l-Fitr (Ramzan),

April 21

 

MahavirJayanti

May 23

 

Buddha Purnima

*June 17

 

Idu'lZuha (Bakrid)

*July 16

 

Muharram

August 15

 

Independence Day

August 26

 

Janmashtami

*September 16

 

Milad-Un-Nabi (Birthday of Prophet Mohammed)

October 02

 

Gandhi Jayanti

October 11

 

Durgashtami

October 13

 

Dussehra (Vijayadasami)

October 31

 

Diwali (Deepavali)

November 15

 

Guru Nanak Jayanti

December 25

 

Christmas Day

 

 

Of these, April 10- Idu’l-Fitr, June 17-Idu'l Zuha (Bakrid), July 16- Muharram, and September 16- Milad-Un-Nabi (Birthday of Prophet Mohammed) have been adopted from the State list. If the State Government changes any of these Holidays, only the changed date will be observed as a Holiday for the Central Government Offices in Kerala

Two of the 41 restricted holidays are available to Central Government employees. These include the following special days in Kerala : January 02- MannamJayanti, January 14- Makarasanranti, March 12- AyyaVaikundaswamy Jayanti , April 13 : Vishu, August 08- KarkidakaVavu, August 20- SreeNarayana Guru Jayanthi, September 07- Ganesh Chathurthi, September 14- First Onam, September 15- ThiruOnam, September 16- Third Onam, September 17 - Fourth Onam September 21- SreeNarayana Guru Samadhi Day.

The decision regarding finalization of the list of holidays to be observed by Central Government Offices in 2024 was taken at a meeting of the Central Government Welfare Employees Coordination Committee in Thiruvananthapuram.
